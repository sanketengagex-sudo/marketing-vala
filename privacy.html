Privacy Policy & Data Transparency

Effective Date: October 2026 • Entity: Marketing Vala (Agency & Automation)

1. Overview & Commitment to Transparency

At Marketing Vala, we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding your personal and business data. This Privacy Policy outlines what information we collect when you visit our website, submit a consultation inquiry, or communicate with us, and how that information is utilized.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect information that you knowingly and voluntarily provide to us through our website consultation forms and direct communication channels. This includes:

  • Contact Information: Full Name, Email Address, Phone Number, and Company/Brand Name.
  • Project Scope: Selected service interest, project brief, budget preferences, and timeframe requirements.
  • Technical Metadata: Lead identification string, submission timestamp, and submission origin for processing and spam prevention.

3. Third-Party Messaging Platform Transparency

Mandatory Notice Regarding WhatsApp & Email Communications:
Clicking our "Chat on WhatsApp" buttons or email links initiates a direct conversation with our team on those respective external platforms (WhatsApp / Gmail). Direct conversations on WhatsApp or native email clients are not automatically logged to this website unless an enterprise webhook/API is explicitly active. We do not claim or pretend that third-party chats are recorded on our web server.

4. How We Use Your Inquiries

Any information submitted through our website is strictly used for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • Reviewing your project specifications and preparing a customized proposal or consultation roadmap.
  • Reaching out via WhatsApp, phone, or email to coordinate strategy discussions.
  • Recording consultation inquiries into our internal Google Sheets lead pipeline (when integrated via secure Google Apps Script).
  • We never sell, lease, or rent your contact information or project ideas to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

5. Testimonials and Case Study Integrity

In accordance with our agency ethics policy, Marketing Vala does not publish fabricated client reviews, manufactured ratings, or artificial client identities. Testimonial spots on this site represent placeholders until genuine reviews are signed off by verified clients upon project completion.

6. Data Security and Retention

We employ sensible organizational and technical measures to protect submitted consultation data against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. Inquiries are retained only as long as necessary to facilitate the consultation and ongoing project relationship.
